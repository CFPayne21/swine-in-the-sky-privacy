Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Swine in the Sky app (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Charles Payne (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not require registration to play. However, it uses LootLocker, a third-party service, to provide online leaderboards. When the Application is used, a player identifier, player name, and game score may be transmitted to and stored by LootLocker to associate scores with players and display leaderboard rankings. LootLocker may also record player activity information, such as when a player was first seen and most recently seen by the service. Player names may be randomly generated by the Application.


Does the Application collect precise real-time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Yes. LootLocker processes information submitted through the online leaderboard and may maintain player profile and activity information to provide its services. This may include player identifiers, player names, game scores, and first-seen and last-seen timestamps. For more information, please review the LootLocker Privacy Policy.


Advertising and Analytics

The Application does not include third-party advertising or use leaderboard information for targeted advertising. The leaderboard feature is used to provide online score rankings.


What are my opt-out rights?

The online leaderboard is a feature of the Application. You can stop all further activity associated with the Application by uninstalling it. Information previously submitted to LootLocker may remain stored according to its data retention practices.


Children

The Application is not intended to knowingly solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Players should avoid including real names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information in leaderboard names. If you believe a child has provided personally identifiable information through the Application, please contact the Service Provider at swineinthesky.support@gmail.com so appropriate action can be considered.


Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Application. Information submitted to the online leaderboard is processed by LootLocker and is subject to its security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.


Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will publish any changes on this page. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
