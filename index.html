Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Swine in the Sky app (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Charles Payne (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not require registration to play. However, it uses LootLocker, a third-party service, to provide online leaderboards. When playing the game, the player's identifier, player name, and game score will be transmitted to and stored by LootLocker to associate scores with players and display leaderboard rankings. Player names are randomly generated when the Application is first started, using the name "Player" followed by a random three-digit number (for example, Player123). Players may change their player name if they wish. Players should not include personally identifiable information, such as their real name, email address, phone number, or other personal details, in their leaderboard name. LootLocker also records player activity information, including when the player was first seen and most recently seen by the service.


Does the Application collect precise real-time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Yes. LootLocker processes information submitted through the online leaderboard and maintains player profile and activity information to provide its services. This includes player identifiers, player names, game scores, and first-seen and last-seen timestamps. For more information, please review the LootLocker Privacy Policy.


Advertising and Analytics

The Application does not include third-party advertising or use leaderboard information for targeted advertising. The leaderboard feature is used to provide online score rankings.


What are my opt-out and data deletion rights?

You can stop using the online leaderboard at any time. Uninstalling the Application stops further activity from that installation, but does not automatically delete information previously submitted to LootLocker. If you would like to request deletion of your leaderboard player profile or associated scores, please contact the Service Provider at swineinthesky.support@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and handled using the available tools and procedures for the online leaderboard service.


Children

The Application is not intended to knowingly solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Players should avoid including real names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information in leaderboard names. If you believe a child has provided personally identifiable information through the Application, please contact the Service Provider at swineinthesky.support@gmail.com so the matter can be reviewed.


Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Application. Information submitted to the online leaderboard is processed by LootLocker and is subject to its security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.


Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will publish any changes on this page. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
